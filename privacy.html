Privacy Policy

SuFO Marketing (SuFO Studios) · Last updated 13 November 2026

What this app is

SuFO Marketing is an internal tool used only by SuFO Studios. It uploads gameplay videos of our own games to our own YouTube channel and reads that channel's analytics. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel.
  • Read-only access to that channel's details and video list.
  • Read-only access to that channel's YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, likes and similar figures).

How data is used and stored

  • Analytics are used only to see how our own videos perform and to plan new ones.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on our own computer, in Windows Credential Manager. Nothing is stored on any server.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • We do not collect data about viewers or any other users.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove this app's access at any time at Google security settings. When access is removed, the stored tokens stop working, and we delete them from our computer.
